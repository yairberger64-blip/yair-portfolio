# THE RIGHT TIME — Master Screenplay (silent)

*A 3D animated short. No dialogue, narration or intelligible speech. On-screen text is limited to place/time cards, name tags and the end title.*
*Target runtime is 5:30. Fact references (C#/R#) point to `fact_ledger.md`.*

---

## CHAPTER 1 — THE INTRODUCTION (0:00–0:38) · *built as previs v001*

**1. INT. HOMETALK OFFICE, JERUSALEM — DAY (2017)**
Warm afternoon light pours through floor-to-ceiling windows over a skyline of Jerusalem stone and rooftop water heaters. The camera glides across the open office. Card: **JERUSALEM · 2017**.

YAIR (early 20s, just out of the army, blue tee) types at his desk, focused. A small win: he hits Enter and allows himself a satisfied smile. *(C1)*

KATELYN (his colleague, mustard sweater, ponytail) crosses the office on a mission, brisk, almost tiptoeing. She taps his shoulder. He swivels his chair round and grins hello. *(C2)*

She bounces, pulls out her phone and holds it up: a photo of **SHARONE** with a heart. Her eyebrows ask the whole question. *(C3, R2)*

Yair smiles politely at the photo... then his eyes drift past her to the coffee corner, where a woman with her hair in a bun is making coffee and tucking a strand behind her ear. *(C4, R3)*

Katelyn follows his gaze, turns back and gives him a flat, half-lidded look. He shrugs sheepishly. She sighs and looks down at her phone.

**2. MATCH CUT — the camera pushes into the phone until Sharone's photo fills the frame...**

**3. INT. SHARONE & KATELYN'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**
...and the photo becomes SHARONE herself, laughing on the couch, wine glass in hand. String lights over the window, the city glittering beyond. *(R4)*

Katelyn, cross-legged beside her, is mid-pitch with both hands moving, one holding her phone and the other her glass. She turns the phone round: a photo of Yair. Sharone leans in, sceptical eyebrow raised... and a small, curious smile wins. A tiny shrug: *maybe.* They clink glasses.

The camera drifts up to the window and the city lights. Fade out.

## CHAPTER 2 — THREE YEARS OF BAD TIMING (0:38–1:40)

A playful split-screen montage. **Katelyn's theme** returns every time she tries again.

- A wall calendar flips: 2017 → 2018. The screen splits into Yair on the left and Sharone on the right.
- A heart icon hops between the panels like a ball that keeps missing: when one side has a partner silhouette, the other is alone, then they swap. *(C9; partners stay faceless silhouettes)*
- Katelyn's phone: message bubbles with no readable text, only emoji and photo thumbnails sent to Yair. *(C7)*
- Yair with a rolling suitcase. An airplane crosses a stylised map from Israel to America. Katelyn's bubbles follow him across time zones as the clock icons change. *(C7)*
- Yair's return flight. A university campus: backpack, lecture hall, notebooks. *(C8)*
- A comic beat: Katelyn tries to "arrange" a meeting, and a calendar block for each of them shows a different week.
- Calendar: 2019 → 2020.

**Tone:** bright, rhythmic, comedic timing on cuts.

## CHAPTER 3 — THE WRONG RELATIONSHIPS (1:40–2:25)

The palette cools and the camera slows. Restrained music: solo cello and sparse piano.

- Yair on a couch beside a faceless, softly lit partner silhouette. They are inches apart, but the space between them reads as a wall.
- Sharone at a café table across from a partner silhouette. A long silence: her untouched cup, the steam fading.
- Body language only: arms crossed, someone turning away, a phone screen lighting a face in the dark. No specific incidents are depicted. *(C10; no villains, no invented events)*
- Each sits alone afterwards. Yair by a window with rain. Sharone on a balcony.
- A slow breath. Each one stands, opens a door and steps into daylight. *Moving forward.*

---

## CHAPTER 4 — FINALLY, THE MEETING (2:25–3:15)

- Card: **2020**. Katelyn wheels her suitcase out of arrivals at the airport, determined. *(C11)*
- She checks two phone contacts and sees both statuses as "single" (a visual gag). Her face: *finally.*
- **Sharone's apartment.** A doorbell. Sharone opens the door. Katelyn is standing there with Yair half a step behind her.
- Natural awkwardness: a hand offered for a shake at the same moment as a half-hug, both of them laughing and resetting. Katelyn hovers, delighted, pours wine and over-hosts.
- A glance held a beat too long, and both of them look away. Katelyn notices and pretends not to.
- **Quiet connection, not instant romance.** The love theme is heard for the first time as a fragment on solo piano: the matchmaker motif slowed.

## CHAPTER 5 — SLOWLY BECOMING US (3:15–4:15)

- Irregular meetings in a montage with uneven gaps: a walk in the Old City alleys (generic), coffee, a market, a late-night drive. A calendar shows the meetings spaced unevenly. *(C12)*
- Light gets warmer and framing gets tighter with each meeting.
- **January 2021** card. The two of them sit on a bench and turn to each other with a "so... what are we?" look, then a shy smile. *(C13)*
- About two months later: a hand reaches out and is taken. *(C14; card to be confirmed, see O1)*
- **July 2021.** An intimate, quiet moment. Sharone looks at him, says something we don't hear, and the music drops out. His face changes, and he answers. *(C15; circumstances kept symbolic)*

---

## CHAPTER 6 — A LIFETIME TOGETHER (4:15–5:00)

- A symbolic wedding image: hands, rings, a canopy of light. No claim to recreate the real day. *(C16, O2)*
- A home fills with life: boxes unpacked, plants growing, photos appearing on a wall.
- A crib. A tiny hand wraps round Yair's finger. **Mia.** *(C16, O3)*
- The family of three: bath-time splash, first steps between them, laughter.
- Full orchestral development of the love theme.

---

## CHAPTER 7 — OUR NEXT CHAPTER (5:00–5:30)

- Present day. A living room half-packed: suitcases, a map with Israel and the US, Mia "helping" by sitting in a suitcase. *(C17)*
- An airport. Mia walks between her parents, holding both their hands, and they look out at a plane at the gate.
- Cut to an aircraft lifting into a sunrise. *(C17 is planned, not completed, so the film ends on departure and not arrival.)*
- Title: **THE RIGHT TIME**
