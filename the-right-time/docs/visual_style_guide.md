# Visual Style Guide

**North star:** a cohesive stylised 3D feature film, not a string of disconnected clips. One lens language, one set of character proportions, one lighting logic per chapter.

## Proportions and materials
- Heads at ×1.3 scale, large eyes with catch-lights, soft rounded limbs, matte materials (roughness 0.5–0.7). No photoreal skin.
- No distorted hands. In previs, hands are simple mitts. In final animation, reject any generation that shows extra or merged fingers.

## Camera
- Lenses as vertical FOV: 26° (long, voyeuristic), 30° (close-ups), 34–36° (two-shots and wides). Avoid wide distortion near faces.
- Motion should be motivated: dolly or push to build intimacy, pan to follow action, crane for endings. Little or no slow motion.
- Cut on action. Keep eyelines consistent (180° rule): in the office Katelyn stays screen-left of Yair after the swivel.

## Typography (on-screen text)
Inter, warm white `#fff8ee` with a soft shadow. Place/time cards bottom-left with a letter-spaced subline. Name tags use a thin rule plus uppercase name. End title: centred, generous tracking.

## Avoid
Lens flares, excessive gradients, glow-everything "AI" looks, hyper-detailed skin, rubbery faces, readable fake UI text.
