## 3 — Katelyn arrives
Keyframe: The same characters as the attached images: identical faces, hair and outfits. In the bright
Jerusalem tech office, the woman leans on the young man's desk beside him, beaming with excitement,
holding up her phone toward him. He has swivelled his chair to face her, smiling, curious.
Two-shot, three-quarter angle, big windows with Jerusalem stone buildings behind. + style suffix
Animate: She bounces slightly on her toes and wiggles the phone at him with raised eyebrows; he leans
in, amused. Gentle camera push. No talking.

## 4 — Phone insert
Keyframe: Close-up of a hand holding a smartphone in a sunny office; the screen shows a photo of the
woman in the attached image smiling, with a small red heart icon. Office softly blurred behind. + style suffix
Animate: The hand gives the phone a little hopeful shake; slow push-in toward the screen. No talking.

## 5 — Yair's eyes drift
Keyframe: The same characters as the attached images. Close-up of the young man looking at the phone
the woman holds, with a polite smile, but his eyes drift past it toward the far side of the office,
where a woman with her hair in a bun stands with her back to camera at a coffee machine (soft focus,
face not visible). + style suffix
Animate: His polite smile slowly turns dreamy as he gazes past the phone. No talking.

## 6 — Katelyn's deadpan
Keyframe: The same character as the attached image, in the office, looking back over her shoulder,
then facing camera with a flat, unimpressed, half-lidded look, phone lowered. Medium close-up. + style suffix
Animate: She glances over her shoulder, turns back with a deadpan stare, lets out a big sigh, shoulders
drop, and looks down at her phone. No talking.

## 7 — Apartment: the pitch (night)
Keyframe: The same characters as the attached images: identical faces, hair and outfits. Evening in a
cosy Jerusalem apartment: the two women sit on a teal couch with glasses of red wine, warm lamplight,
string lights over a window showing city lights at night. The honey-haired woman talks animatedly and
turns her phone toward the dark-haired woman, who is laughing. Medium two-shot. + style suffix
Animate: The honey-haired woman gestures enthusiastically with her phone; the dark-haired woman laughs,
then raises a sceptical eyebrow and sips her wine. No talking.

## 8 — Apartment: "maybe" + clink
Keyframe: Same characters and apartment as shot 7. The dark-haired woman looks at the phone with a
small, curious smile; the honey-haired woman watches her hopefully. Closer two-shot. + style suffix
Animate: The dark-haired woman gives a tiny shrug and a growing smile; they clink their wine glasses.
Camera slowly drifts toward the window and the city lights. No talking.
