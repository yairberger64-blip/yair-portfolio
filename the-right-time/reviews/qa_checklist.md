# QA Checklist (run per shot, then per cut)

- [ ] **Identity**: each character matches the bible, with consistent hair, skin and wardrobe within the scene
- [ ] **Faces**: no distortion, eyes aligned, mouth readable, blinks natural
- [ ] **Hands and body**: no extra or merged fingers, no limb intersections with props or furniture
- [ ] **Temporal**: no popping, flicker, morphing or texture crawl
- [ ] **Camera continuity**: 180° rule, eyelines match across cuts, screen direction held
- [ ] **Lighting**: key direction and colour match the chapter palette and the neighbouring shots
- [ ] **Environment**: set dressing consistent across angles
- [ ] **Audio sync**: taps, steps, clink and chime land within ±1 frame of picture
- [ ] **No dialogue**: no speech, narration or intelligible vocals (mouth movement is fine, audio is not)
- [ ] **Historical accuracy**: nothing contradicts `docs/fact_ledger.md`; reconstructions stay non-specific
